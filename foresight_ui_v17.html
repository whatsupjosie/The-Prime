The Diner

The rain kept tapping the window in that slow, deliberate rhythm.

Sahira slid the envelope across the table without looking up.

“You don't have to open it here,” she said.

Repeat turned it over once, feeling the weight of whatever was inside.

“I know,” he said. “I'm going to anyway.”

He peeled back the flap slowly, like it might still change its mind.

For a second neither of them said anything at all.

Then he folded it back along its original crease.
